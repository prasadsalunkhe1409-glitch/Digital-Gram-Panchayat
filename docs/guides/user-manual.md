# Citizen User Manual

The Digital Gram Panchayat app lets you access Grampanchayat services from your phone, in
**English or Marathi**, online or offline.

## Getting the app

- Open the citizen web address in a mobile browser.
- Tap **Install** when prompted (or browser menu → "Add to Home screen") to use it like a
  native app, offline-ready.
- Choose your language any time from **Settings** or the top bar (मराठी / English).

## Create an account

1. Open **Register**.
2. Enter your name, 10-digit mobile number, a password, village, and address.
3. Tap **Register** — you're signed in and receive a welcome notification.
   Next time, use **Log in** with your mobile number and password.

## File a complaint

1. **Complaints → New**.
2. Pick a category (Road, Water Supply, Sanitation, Electricity, Other).
3. Enter a subject and description; optionally add photos and capture your GPS location.
4. Tap **Submit**. You'll get a complaint ID (e.g. `CMP-2026-000123`).

> **Offline?** The complaint is saved and sent automatically when you reconnect — no duplicates.

## Track complaints

**Complaints** lists all your complaints with status (Pending → In Progress → Resolved). Tap
one to see its timeline and officer remarks. You're notified when the status changes.

## Apply for a certificate (Dakhala)

1. **Certificates → Apply**.
2. Choose a type (Marriage, Birth, Death, 7/12 & 8A, Other).
3. Fill the required fields and attach supporting documents.
4. Submit — track status (Submitted → Under Review → Approved/Rejected).
5. When **Approved**, open the application and tap **Download certificate** (PDF).

## View tax dues

**Tax** shows your property/water tax by year — assessed, paid, and balance. Filter by year.

## Notices & schemes

- **Notices:** announcements from the Grampanchayat (search + filter by category).
- **Schemes:** government schemes with eligibility, documents, benefits, and how to apply.
  Both are readable offline once viewed.

## Notifications

The bell icon shows updates (complaint/certificate/tax changes, broadcasts). Tap to read; use
**Mark all read** to clear the badge. Choose channels in **Settings → Notifications**.

## Settings

Language, appearance (light/dark), notification preferences, Help & FAQ, and **Replay
onboarding** for the intro tour.

## Tips

- Works offline for reading saved data; new complaints queue and sync later.
- If a screen fails to load, use the **Retry** button.
- Keep the app updated — tap **Reload** when the "new version available" message appears.
